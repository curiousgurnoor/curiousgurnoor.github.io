## Notes
”It took us decades to implement electronification of financial markets after the 1960s Paperwork Crisis, so the tokenization of markets are an inevitable future.”

Today’s global financial system is dependent on trusted centralized intermediaries and fragmented message-based integrations to reconcile ownership and transfers across siloed networks. Through tokenization, we transition from fixed-time regional trading markets to global continuous trading markets. We need blockchains as layers of digital property rights to be recognized to enable the final frontier of tokenization: **native asset issuance onchain** .

Assuming that we are able to break down the cost barriers of interacting with and composing financial assets, financial engineering becomes much more free: financialengineering/acc. With this explosion of potential financial assets along a continuous risk-reward curve, instead of a discrete one, we need a curation and distribution interface for discovery. **Price** is the first filter, and **Liquidity** is the second filter. We can separate this into two layers: _Curation of the Underlying Asset (What is the actually asset?)_ and _Instrumentation of the Asset (How is it represented and distributed?)_

### The value proposition of tokenization is:
- Programmable Settlement

- Fractional Asset Ownership

- Asset Composability (Multi-Asset Mobility)

- Flexible Collateral Assets

- Platform-Specific Stablecoins as Settlement Assets

- Enhancement of Information Symmetry

- Flexible Custodial Agreements

### Use Cases of Tokenization
- Net New Asset Issuance such as Fixed Income Debt (Sovereign, Corporate, Municipal), Public Equities, Private Equity, Commodities, Real Estate, and Securities Financing such as Collateral and Repurchase Agreement Markets. Assets that have opaque OTC market infrastructure is prime for tokenization.

- Tokenized public equities will resolve the issue of corporate actions programmed into the asset. Corporate actions are capital markets’ largest unstructured data challenge.

- Collateral can exist onchain, but to do so, it should be free from liquidity and credit risk. What could be pretty cool is the **mobility and utility of collateral** through tokenization.

Greater clarity in fund composition and transparent ownership will enhance the ability for tokenized strucutred products to be used as productive collateral.

**How will the following roles evolve with tokenization?**

- **Buy/Sell-Side Funds:** Their role expands as they can construct/purchase tokenized assets.

- **Issuers:** Enhanced operational efficiency in the issuance process of tokenized assets.

- **Exchanges:** It will be interesting to see here how CEXs and DEXs merge.

- **Centralized Counterparties:** May specialize in concentrating liquidity and safely conducting trades as brokers - Collateral Managers.

- **Central Security Depositories:**

- **Custodians:** Vertically-integrated custody, staking, multi-sig, and institutional wallets.

## Questions
- How do DEXs and CEXs merge in regards to sharing liquidity, asset types, and making them composable across one another?

- What is the issuance and trading lifecycle of equities, fixed income assets, private equity, commodities, real estate, and other securities financing assets?

- What would settlement onchain look like, and is it a naturally centralized process?

- Why would we ever need to exist in a backed token model? For region-specific investors that already have access to the asset in question off-chain, tokenization is just a nice frontend. However, backed token models could be vital for global markets where you may require trusted custodians to hold an asset, or for commodities that require physical settlement.

- • What was the transition from paper securities to de-materialization like? How did they resolve reconciliation between the two different ledgers in the transition period?

- Could platform-specific stablecoins be a potential settlement asset? I am assuming we will need hyper-efficient AMMs that can swap between different platform-specific stablecoins.

- Do you see each participant in the sell-side market launching their own chain? Why would J.P Morgan want to settle transactions on the Blackrock chain or in the Blackrock settlement asset? Or is there some middle-ground with interoperability or an agreed-upon third-party institutional chain?

- What factors contribute to discounted value in tokenized versions of assets? There is a lack of onchain redemptions and liquidations, and there is barely any secondary market liquidity. There is also a lack of incentives for market makers to provide liquidity in tokenized market, especially with the current dual-structure liquidity venues.
